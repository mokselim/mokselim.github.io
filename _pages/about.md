I am a neuroscientist and biophysicist. I work as a **Researcher and Preclinical MRI Research Facility Manager** at the [Instituto de Neurociencias CSIC-UMH](https://in.umh-csic.es/) in Alicante, Spain. I am also a Research Associate at the Sir Peter Mansfield Imaging Centre (University of Nottingham, UK) and at the Schulich School of Medicine & Dentistry (University of Western Ontario, Canada).

My research combines machine learning, multimodal neuroimaging, microscopy, immunohistochemistry and gut microbiota data to study neurological and psychiatric disorders, including alcohol use disorder, aging and Alzheimer's disease. I work across clinical and preclinical models, using diffusion MRI, tractography and brain connectomics to understand how the brain changes in health and disease.

Research interests
======
* Mental disorders (alcohol use disorder, ...)
* Computational neuroimaging and MRI
* Brain connectomics and tractography
* Preclinical models
* Neuro-inspired systems

Background
======
I received my PhD in Neurosciences (Summa Cum Laude, International Doctorate) from the Instituto de Neurociencias CSIC-UMH in 2024 as a 'la Caixa' Foundation & MSCA-COFUND doctoral fellow. My thesis was *Characterising White Matter Disruption in Alcohol Use Disorder: A Deep TMS Intervention Study*. I then worked as a Research Fellow at the University of Nottingham (2024–2025) within the NIH BRAIN Initiative Centre for Mesoscale Connectomics. Before that, I earned a Master in Neurosciences from Miguel Hernández University (2018) and a Bachelor of (Bio)Physics from Al-Azhar University (2016), where I ranked first in my class.

At the MRI facility I advise new and current users on how to use MRI methods to answer their neuroscience questions. I also teach in the Master's Programme in Neuroscience at Miguel Hernández University and at the NeurotechEU Summer School, and I mentor undergraduate and graduate trainees.
